## Writing to me

- [@mario_ttide](https://t.me/mario_ttide) for anything.
- [@mariolimitatibot](https://t.me/mariolimitatibot) if Telegram limits you
  and you cannot write in private to someone who does not have you in their
  contacts.
- [@SuperMarioBots](https://t.me/SuperMarioBots) is the channel with the
  updates.

When you report a problem, three things are needed: which bot (the exact
username, if it is a clone), in which group, and what happens instead of what
you expect. A screenshot is worth more than a description.

## Custom bots

Besides the public bots I write bots and userbots to order. My most used
public bot, [@Tagga_Bot](https://t.me/Tagga_Bot), has been cloned more than
500 times and is on over 23 thousand chats.

Every bot runs on a server with daily backups, monitoring and a central
database. If you would rather keep your bot on a server of your own, I give
you the sources and the instructions, and I keep supplying the updates
without having access to your machine. It is the best option for privacy, but
it needs Linux and the command line.

## Supporting the project

The bots are free, the server is not. It is a donation and nothing else: it
unlocks no features, it gives no priority and it changes nothing in the bot.

- **Telegram Stars**: open [@VetrinaSuperMarioBot](https://t.me/VetrinaSuperMarioBot)
  and press **❤️ Support ❤️**. You can pick 100, 250 or 500 Stars.
- **PayPal**: [paypal.me/SuperMarioBots](https://paypal.me/SuperMarioBots).

The same **Support** button is inside some of the bots, with the same two
options.
